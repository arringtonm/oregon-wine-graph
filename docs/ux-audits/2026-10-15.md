# UX audit, 15 October 2026 (v1.12)

The first full audit with `.claude/skills/ux-audit/`.

- **Methods:** Nielsen Norman Group heuristics, visual hierarchy, icon usability and severity ratings.
- **What was checked:**
  - captures of the production build at 1512, 1024, 820, 393 and 360px, in light and dark, for the start view, The Eyrie Vineyards and Temperance Hill;
  - the drawer, the Controls guide and other popovers, checked at 393px in the browser.
- **Severity:** 0 not a problem, 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major, 4 catastrophe.

## Fixed

| Sev | Principle | Where | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Consistency; aesthetic design | Controls guide, phones | The panel was a fixed 30rem (420px), wider than a 393px screen, so its key labels and platform label were cut off at the right. Every other popover limits itself to the screen. | `width: min(30rem, 100vw − 2rem)`, the available-height cap and 12px collision padding (`ControlsGuide.vue`). Rechecked at 393px: 16px margins each side. |

## Notes on method

- Headless Chrome won't lay a page out narrower than 500px. The capture script puts phone widths in an exact-width frame.
- In that frame the graph isn't drawn and the lazily loaded Ask button can be missing. Both were confirmed fine at 393px in the real browser.
